# mississippi-divide-index-public# Mississippi Divide Index

A thematic equity index dividing the S&P 500 by the Mississippi River — capturing the structural economic divergence between East and West United States.

---

## Thesis

Companies headquartered **West of the Mississippi River** have structurally outperformed due to:
- Lower state taxes and lighter regulation
- Tech sector concentration (CA, WA, TX)
- Energy independence and faster GDP growth
- Stronger population and business formation trends

## Methodology

The index follows a **proprietary, rules-based quantitative approach**:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Universe** | S&P 500 constituents |
| **Classification** | HQ state East or West of the Mississippi River |
| **Selection** | Quantitative ranking with liquidity screens |
| **Weighting** | Market-cap based with diversification constraints |
| **Rebalancing** | Systematic, calendar-based |
